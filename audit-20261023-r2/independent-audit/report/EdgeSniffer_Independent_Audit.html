Independent audit of EdgeSniffer

Source, causal inference, market microstructure, execution economics, and a prioritized research program

22 October 2026 | Read-only research audit | Source bundle SHA-256: f6ff8036cbccf76e4ab6b8ba61969ac5e915098de03dc900a6282e0b7338ba27

Decision

No strategy in this archive can be called genuinely profitable or independently executable. The evidence supports continued research, but not the premise that the remaining task is to find a better exit or one final filter. Several components do not measure the same signal, clock, state, or population. Reported gains are conditional marks on selected observations; they are not a complete account of independent attempts and liquidation.

The single best next build is a small, versioned causal opportunity-and-attempt ledger with one shared feature function and a deterministic execution-model replay. It should repair the measurement path, reuse the strongest existing components, and immediately test whether any net edge survives a one-second delay. Demote the current frozen teacher classifier to a benchmark until its native implementation has parity and its full-market economic value is measured.

This audit recommends no live trading. A successful next phase would establish causally reproducible, execution-model-consistent shadow results. That is a necessary research gate; it does not itself demonstrate real order landing, fills, or liquidation.

Five conclusions to act on

• Confirmed facts: the preserved scorer is intact, its report is explicitly development-only, and all 76 assessed selected external states precede the teacher transaction’s recorded receipt by less than one second. The median receipt lead is only 36.27 ms. The raw market tapes needed to reprice the results are not in the archive.

• Implementation blockers: the native scorer compares a logit to a probability threshold; sell flow can become zero; current instruction variants are omitted; instruction budgets can be mistaken for fills; state ordering and checkpoint publication can change outcomes; failed entries and unresolved exits leave the economic denominator.

• Hypotheses worth testing: residual continuation after a real one-second delay; sustained multi-wallet participation after a two-second confirmation; sell-pressure recovery; and, if the early-curve economics fail, slower signals in properly modeled deeper pools.

• Invalid or too optimistic results: teacher classification accuracy, best-exit hindsight, fast-checkpoint subsets, closed-and-markable-only PnL, and selected winners from large policy grids do not establish independent profitability. Some negative results are also unreliable because bugs can erase activity or exaggerate liquidity failure.

• Best next build: an append-only observation/state/opportunity/attempt/position ledger, shared feature and integer quote functions, independent exit timers, versioned protocol support, own-trade state propagation, and clean-versus-incremental replay invariants. No broad architecture rewrite is needed.

1. Scope and evidence standard

The supplied archive contains 573 files totaling 9,941,875 uncompressed bytes: 12 research artifacts, 30 Rust-sidecar files, and 531 script files. All 508 Python source files were inventoried and parsed successfully. Deep review focused on the active native decoder/state/lineage paths, frozen scorer and its historical builders, window/control/checkpoint materializers, independent strategy lab, latency study, and older exact-curve/N350 engines. This is a source and research-design audit, not a claim that every line of every historical script was dynamically exercised.

The archive omits the raw native transaction tape, account seed stream, teacher episode input tapes, historical finalized transactions, complete economics output tables, later allocation manifests, runtime service configuration, and deployed binary. Therefore historical PnL, deployed bug incidence, full-market alert rates, exact holdout integrity, and actual order execution cannot be independently certified here. Source behavior and synthetic counterexamples can be established; their historical financial impact generally cannot.

Across the three focused workstreams, 53 bundled unittest cases passed, plus a standalone Yellowstone bridge integration check. Separate adversarial fixtures nevertheless reproduced the defects in this report. Passing the existing tests mainly confirms their current assertions; it does not establish the missing causal, scoring, state, and denominator invariants. A complete Rust build was blocked by omitted local modules and dependencies referenced by Cargo. No network-facing collectors or executors from the archive were run. Source files were not modified.

The temporal label is the final external state before a later teacher entry, contrasted with earlier states in that teacher episode. The cross-sectional label is the mint the teacher chose at a teacher-chosen clock. Those are useful behavioral targets. Neither is a label for positive net return after an independent delay, nor a sample of all opportunities available to a separate strategy.

The distinction is especially consequential here: entry at selected-state time plus 1,000 ms occurs after the teacher transaction’s recorded receipt in all 76 assessed episodes. These are receive-clock comparisons, not proof of actual chain execution order. That does not prove the signal has no value. The earlier first-crossing cases need a separate all-market false-alert and economic study.

Class-balanced training gives positives and negatives artificial weights, so the sigmoid output is not a calibrated natural-market probability of teacher action, much less probability of profit. 3. Critical implementation findings

F01 — The native adapter is not the preserved scorer

The adapter returns a raw linear predictor and compares it directly to a probability cutoff. The preserved cutoff is 0.4622968637; its correct logit equivalent is −0.1510993697. The implemented comparison instead implies probability 0.6135589144. Using the actual frozen coefficients, a feature vector yielding probability 0.5 passes the frozen model and fails the native adapter.

The event boundary also changes. Training includes the triggering external transaction and uses its post-state; native scoring sees its pre-state and excludes its flow until after scoring. A synthetic 1 SOL buy contributes 1 SOL of 250 ms buy flow in training and zero in the adapter. The adapter further omits crossMintModel selection and the <=120 second age gate, scores only buys, and can repeat after 300 seconds despite a first-crossing-per-lifecycle description. Its contract is exploratory, so the precise finding is failure to implement the preserved composite policy, not proof of a deployed freeze violation.

Sources: scripts/adapt2CQFrozenScorerNativeV1.py:145–152,183,201–249; twoCqEventDrivenFingerprintFreezeV1.py:184–190,211–215; twoCqDecisionSystemDecompositionV1.py:382–390,410–426. Probes: scorer score_units; native feature-boundary case.

F02 — Ranking metrics can make a constant classifier look perfect

Top-1 uses one plus the count of strictly greater scores, granting every tied maximum rank one. Average precision sorts score/label pairs so positives come first on ties. With one positive and nine negatives, all score 0.5, the implementation reports top-1 100% and AP 1.0; randomized top-1 and threshold-grouped AP are 10% and 0.1. AUC correctly remains 0.5. The amount of inflation in the actual assessment is unknown because complete score arrays are absent. Later code rejects ties, making metric-version consistency another requirement.

Sources: scripts/twoCqCausalEntryTimingFingerprintV1.py:571–604; twoCqPrimaryPolicySealedValidationV1.py:117–135; twoCqN350FrozenPolicyEconomicsSandboxV1.py:105–112. Probes: scorer ranking_ties and average_precision_ties.

F03 — Native signed flow can have the wrong sign

The Rust decoder provides a sell token amount but no sell quote amount. State replay changes reserves without restoring the realized sell quote; downstream features read the missing quote as zero. A 0.277008311 SOL sell followed by two 0.1 SOL buys is reported as +0.2 SOL net flow and passes the burst gate. Signed gross flow is actually −0.077008311 SOL. This directly compromises the mechanism the current burst hypothesis relies on.

Sources: rust-sidecar/src/main.rs:21351–21368,733–760; scripts/materializeNativePumpCurveStateV1.py:55–69,259–294; materialize2CQEventDrivenIndependentWindowsV1.py:71–79; adapt2CQFrozenScorerNativeV1.py:119. Probe: native sell-flow reversal.

F04 — Instructions, intentions, and executed transitions are conflated

main.rs recognizes buy_exact_quote_in_v3 but omits buy_v3 and sell_v3. Current official Pump documentation lists all three and permits mixed legacy/V2/V3 activity on a coin. Even if entries are restricted to one variant, all state-changing activity must be captured or the subsequent state must be quarantined. Static-account-only extraction and top-level-only instruction coverage also threaten address-table and CPI paths.

For legacy/V2 exact-token buys, the decoder reads max_sol_cost as quote spent, and replay uses it as a fill. A transaction intention or maximum budget is not an executed amount. The native replay also accepts an explicitly failed synthetic transaction, changes reserves, and labels economics state complete. A separate Yellowstone subscription filtering failed transactions is a useful local safeguard; it does not certify the Jito path or every downstream input.

Current protocol details make a versioned transition engine necessary. Ordinary V3 trades retain documented price/fee behavior but change fee custody; synthetic migration can split a final buy across curve and future-pool components, after which ordinary curve trading is unavailable until migration. Signed virtual quote-reserve adjustments documented for PumpSwap pools must not be blindly applied as a BondingCurve field reinterpretation.

Sources: rust-sidecar/src/main.rs:77–89,21262–21288,21339–21368,21423–21444,21499–21509; scripts/materializeNativePumpCurveStateV1.py. Official references P1–P4 in section 11. Probes: failed intent; selector inventory.

F05 — CREATE lineage and active-curve status are not enforced end to end

Rust emits nativeKind="creation"; Python state replay recognizes "create". The actual emitted schema followed by a buy yields no creation seed. Fixing the vocabulary alone is insufficient: the creation object omits creator identity, initial reserves, and original instruction evidence. A launch bridge accepts incomplete provenance and can label it exact. Separately, raw account normalization tests complete before decoding it and does not recheck; replay then drops the completion flag. A completed synthetic curve is accepted as an economics-complete buy.

Exact launch features need genuine available-at CREATE lineage. Recent-flow strategies can explicitly omit age, creator, and launch-relative features and use complete recent history plus authoritative current state. Do not make reconstruction of every teacher feature a prerequisite for all research, and do not replace true CREATE time with first observed trade time.

F06 — Ordering and late seeds can erase market transitions

Jito transaction indices restart per received batch, while replay deduplicates by slot/transaction-index/instruction-index without signature. Distinct signatures sharing the tuple can be discarded. Later-arriving earlier-index trades are also discarded. Sorting the already damaged output cannot recover them. A separate late-seed path overwrites current reserves with an older-slot account snapshot without replaying intervening transitions.

In the late-seed reproduction, two 1 SOL buys should reach 31.975 virtual SOL under the module’s own formula. A delayed seed between them produces 30.9875 SOL, erasing 0.9875 SOL. Account seeds lose write-version/signature precision in intermediate handling. Same-slot writes, duplicate delivery, late arrival, and transaction atomicity need explicit identities and reconciliation; file order is not chain order.

Sources: rust-sidecar/src/main.rs:19674–19687; scripts/materializeNativePumpCurveStateV1.py:98–160,217,233–250. Probes: duplicate tuple with distinct signatures; late-seed reset.

F07 — Availability labels do not prove causal availability

The generic native boundary accepts a boolean, source string, and state dictionary without checking that the state or fees were available by entry time. A signal at 1,000 ms, intended entry at 2,000 ms, and explicitly finalized state available at 900,000 ms passes. Normal materializers often select earlier rows; this counterexample establishes guard weakness, not that every produced window leaks.

Historical reconstruction assigns finalized TradeEvent information to an earlier compact-arrival timestamp. The finalized enrichment bridge also carries an old receive clock alongside later fetched state. Such records may be correct ex-post labels but unavailable strategy inputs. The temporal teacher example builder demonstrably accepts selected state at 1100 ms and CREATE received at 1500 ms, then clamps the negative age to zero. The cross-mint builder has a separate CREATE<=boundary check, so the same proof cannot explain all age-zero observations.

The native receive clock is assigned during decoding after batch deserialization and duplicated as decode-complete time. That is not original ingress time. It can be conservative, but cannot substantiate microsecond network lead. Use separate ingress, decode, execution-status, feature-ready, and finality clocks, with whole-transaction observation boundaries.

F08 — Failed entries and unresolved positions leave economic accounting

eligible() ends by checking whether the buy quote exists. The evaluator silently skips a failed quote. A synthetic eligible opportunity with insufficient real-token inventory produces zero outcomes, zero surfaces, and no rejection count. Larger sizes can therefore change the population instead of exposing capacity failures.

PnL, win rate, profit factor, and drawdown use only ACTIONABLE exits. One +2.192002151 SOL outcome plus eight unresolved 1 SOL positions reports +2.192002151 SOL, 100% wins, and zero drawdown. If all eight were total losses, net would be −5.807997849 SOL before other costs. This is a sensitivity bound, not a claim the positions were worthless. An incomplete-case positive PnL is not portfolio profit.

Sources: scripts/runIndependentStrategyLabV1.py:247–268,382–408,429–442; runNativeLatencyBoundedStudyV1.py:56–80,126–140. Probes: failed-entry omission; unresolved-position bound.

F09 — Checkpoint delay depends on processing schedule

The checkpoint process consumes a global account-seed cursor, discards seeds for windows not yet published, and permanently advances. A window published later cannot recover its first qualifying state. On identical final files, incremental processing picks 60,000 ms for a deadline at 17,000 ms and reports 43 seconds of delay; clean replay picks 17,000 ms and reports zero delay. The broad window refresher defaults to 60 seconds, while horizons include 5/10/15 seconds. Historical incidence is unknown without saved cursors and raw streams.

A second conceptual problem remains after that fix: the next account mutation is not the earliest possible liquidation. An old state can still be current if account continuity and no mutation are established. At the exit timer, emit an intent using verified available state; then model landing. Do not wait for another trader to update the account and call that wait execution latency.

Sources: scripts/materializeNativeReserveHorizonCheckpointsV1.py:227–303; refresh2CQFingerprintMarketWindowsV1.py:27,32–53; runNativeLatencyBoundedStudyV1.py:130–144. Probe: identical-file incremental/clean divergence.

F10 — Window and exit rules can use the wrong information set

The broad materializer suppresses duplicate signals with continue before appending the actual market event. Repeated qualifying buys disappear from the market history. Broad and event-driven windows also expire at signal+300 seconds, while a one-second delayed FIXED_300S exit needs signal+301 seconds: strict long-horizon missingness is structurally manufactured.

FIXED_CARRY first uses any later mark if one eventually exists, and carries the earlier state only when none exists anywhere in the file. Appending a 300-second mark changes a previously closed 17-second decision from −0.084952088 to −0.589086725 SOL. A genuine timer policy cannot make that decision using future file contents. Several dynamic exits additionally trigger and fill on the same mark with zero delivery delay.

Broad continuation features use activity between signal and the nominal delayed entry. That can be a legitimate confirmation rule, but its decision clock is then the end of that interval. It cannot observe the whole interval and still receive a fill at its endpoint with the original one-second delay already spent.

F11 — Current reserve marks omit our own intervention and some costs

The buy helper returns post_state, but the lab keeps only token quantity. It later sells into the unchanged historical reserve path. Partial sales can reuse the same liquidity twice. In a flat synthetic market with zero real SOL before our buy, the historical-state exit fails, while a self-consistent buy-and-sell works and loses normal fees: −0.002479139 / −0.012355683 / −0.024701361 SOL at 0.1 / 0.5 / 1 SOL. A separate two-leg example overstates receipts by 0.01435385 SOL when it fails to update reserves after the first leg. These errors are not uniformly optimistic.

Missing fee fields become zero in one path and 125 bps in another; explicit zero is overwritten by an “or 125” default. Historical fee_basis_points can mean protocol-only while native uses aggregate, so an event with 95 protocol plus 30 creator bps can be interpreted as total 95. Fixed 0.00001 SOL round-trip network cost does not capture all configurable priority fees, tips, failed attempts, or account actions. Account rent needs cash-lock and recovery accounting rather than automatic classification as a permanent fee.

The apparent >=2 SOL reserve filter reads virtual SOL, not real quote liquidity. A virtual-30/real-zero state passes. The reviewed materializers leave entry_impact_bps null, so the <=100 bps impact filter rejects everything. Costs and impact must be recomputed by size. Adding a proportional “stress fee” does not simulate landing drift, min-out violation, or failure.

F12 — Controls, cross-sectional clocks, and registration need repair

The scorer adapter calls every non-crossing buy a control, including above-threshold continuation states on the same mint. A synthetic signal and three controls share its future trajectory. The separate five-minute-bucket control builder chooses controls from the original candidate set, leaves them in the candidate comparison, and does not enforce its accumulated used_mints set. Neither is a clean independent no-signal baseline.

Autonomous ranking computes each mint’s features at its own last event rather than the common decision tick. A mint last active at 1100 ms retains 2 SOL of “250 ms” buying and zero recency when ranked at 5000 ms; correct common-clock values are zero recent flow and 3900 ms recency. That script also removes teacher trades from reserve evolution. Teacher identity must not trigger entry, but actual teacher transactions still change the market state.

The latency study loads a contract but executes hardcoded grids. A synthetic contract declaring size 42 SOL, delay 9999 ms, horizon 888 seconds, and an unknown arm is accepted while the code produces the standard 567-cell surface. A file called a contract is not pre-registration enforcement. The generic lab admits up to 7 filters ×27 exits ×3 sizes ×3 stress levels=1,701 cells. These are correlated comparisons, not 1,701 independent tests, but choosing among them still consumes data.

5. A credible economic and causal contract

Two clocks, one explicit decision boundary

Maintain chain order separately from local evidence availability. Each input needs slot/block identity, transaction signature, instruction/inner-instruction identity, account write version, ingress time, decode completion, execution-status availability, commitment/fork status, and source coverage. Features at decision time may use only inputs available by that time. Finalized reconciliation can label the eventual outcome; it cannot retroactively grant information to the decision.

For the first independent experiment, use a successful processed whole transaction plus the required native state as the observation boundary. This is slower than seeing a shred intention but has clearer semantics. Preserve raw-shred prediction as a separate future hypothesis. Processed success is not final settlement: retain dropped-fork and later-reconciliation outcomes against the original opportunity rather than deleting them.

Let t_d be when the final feature and admission decision is available. The primary modeled entry landing is no earlier than t_d+1,000 ms. Price against the state valid at that specified modeled landing boundary, carrying the latest prior state only when continuity is proven. Bracket same-slot or transaction-order uncertainty. Missing state makes execution unresolved; do not silently move landing to the next snapshot. If policy confirmation or a cancellation criterion observes new data at t_d+1,000 ms, that becomes a new decision and needs its own subsequent delivery interval. Entry and exit delays are separate.

Quote and inventory semantics

Track program/mode/version, quote and base mint/decimals/token program, virtual and real reserves, supply, completion/migration state, fee configuration and validity interval, retained/claimable fees where relevant, account ownership/discriminator, and state provenance. CREATE is mandatory only for features that depend on true creation or creator history. Every state-changing route must be decoded or reconciled before its state is admissible, even when entries use a narrower supported route.

For each size, inject our buy into a separate counterfactual reserve and inventory path, then replay later external instructions subject to their constraints. Propagate partial sells and every fee/leg. Do not pretend external traders would behave identically after a large intervention: report induced external constraint failures and the resulting model-dependence. Show the historical-state small-trader approximation separately as a diagnostic, not as the execution truth.

Account for actual version-specific protocol/creator/LP fees and rounding, network base and priority fees, tips, failed landed submissions, token-account actions, and recoverable rent. Charge nothing automatically for a never-processed hypothetical drop unless the declared route actually incurs that cost. Current Solana documentation states a 5,000-lamport base fee per signature and priority fees based on requested compute-unit limit and price; this does not justify a universal total cost for an entire strategy.

Default research execution rules

Use 0.1 SOL as the primary notional; 0.5 and 1 SOL are correlated same-opportunity capacity scenarios. Freeze the decision-time quote and instruction constraints. A defensible initial, deliberately unoptimized constraint is min-output equal to 99% of that quote, with a maximum 5% average entry curve impact; explicitly report all guard rejections. These are starting protocol choices to pre-register, not discovered optimal tolerances. Cost stress of +25/+50 bps per side is a separate sensitivity, not a substitute for state-path execution. Also run 2-second entry/exit landing stress.

For fixed exits, the timer starts at modeled entry fill. At the deadline create a full-liquidation intent against the verified available state, then apply a separate 1,000 ms exit delivery interval. Use the same 1% min-output convention. A failed or unconfirmed sell leaves open inventory. Permit at most one preregistered retry after a confirmed failure and a new available quote, with its own 1,000 ms delay and fee; absent reliable failure timing, retain an unresolved outcome instead of inventing a retry. Record the first sellable-inventory boundary. A timer does not confer a fill.

Every opportunity retains statuses for guard rejection, modeled unfilled order, processed failure, modeled fill, open inventory, closed inventory, and unknown execution. Report cash, realized PnL, liquidation-value bounds, locked capital, and opportunity cost. A conservative zero-recovery scenario is useful as a lower-bound sensitivity; it is not an assertion of actual zero recovery. If admissible valuation uncertainty changes the sign, profitability is unresolved.

6. The single best next engineering build

Build a read-only, versioned causal opportunity-and-attempt ledger plus a shared replay engine. Limit the first implementation to one explicitly supported SOL-quoted Pump mode. Do not delay all research for a universal chain parser, but do not silently ignore unsupported transitions that affect an admitted mint. Quarantine those intervals and report the excluded universe before outcomes are examined.

Acceptance tests before economic interpretation

• Shared-feature parity: identical event boundary, history, common clock and state produce identical features, score domains, cutoff decisions, age gates, and lifecycle deduplication in offline and native paths. Include ties and both buy/sell triggers.

• Protocol parity: executed amounts, fee splits, reserve transitions and completion/migration match authoritative fixtures at integer precision, allowing only documented rounding. Use at least 10,000 retained transitions across at least 500 mints if available, plus explicit rare-mode/failure/same-slot/CPI/lookup-table fixtures. Counts are coverage targets, not a substitute for enumerating supported modes.

• Causal prefix invariance: appending future evidence cannot change earlier decisions, features, admissions or exit intents. Later labels may reconcile outcomes while preserving the original information set.

• Replay determinism: clean, streamed, delayed-window, duplicate-delivery, late-seed, partial-line, and restart schedules produce the same canonical decisions and ledger, or explicitly bracket unresolved chain order. Never silently resolve uncertainty with future success.

• Conservation and denominator invariants: every input opportunity is reconciled to a status; inventory/cash/fees balance; no failed quote disappears; no completed or unsupported curve becomes active by label; candidates and controls have comparable evidence availability.

• Forward-coverage invariant: a quiet state is valid only with evidence that the relevant feed remained complete. Missing future records never manufacture an EXACT horizon endpoint.

Deliver one parity report, an immutable opportunity/attempt ledger, and a one-page data-quality and economic funnel. This build is complete when it can falsify a simple delayed strategy on a trustworthy population. It need not reproduce every teacher-specific feature before doing that.

T1. Delayed-edge decomposition — the highest-value strategy diagnostic

Use preserved teacher episodes only as hypothesis-generation diagnostics, and independently triggered episodes wherever complete native tapes exist. Reconstruct movement before signal, between signal and teacher, between signal and modeled landing, and after landing. Compare the repaired frozen score, a lifecycle/recency baseline, and the simple burst rule. Teacher-selected clocks remain explicitly labeled teacher-conditioned; they cannot certify a runnable strategy.

Primary specification: independent t_d+1,000 ms entry, 0.1 SOL, full exit at entry fill+15 seconds followed by 1,000 ms exit delivery. Capacity: 0.5/1 SOL on the same opportunities. Delay diagnostics: 0/250/500/1,000/2,000/5,000 ms; 5/60-second holds are descriptive only, not alternative winners. Use exact fees, own-state replay, constraints and all attempts. Show a generous best-executable-exit hindsight bound under the declared replay model only as a falsification ceiling: a negative upper bound is useful rejection evidence; a positive one is not a strategy.

Controls: eligible mints at the same independent decision clock, matched on venue/mode, fee epoch, predecision depth, known age band when applicable, baseline volatility and capture health. Do not match away the burst variable being tested. Existing N350 remains exploratory. Aim for 300–500 independent mint episodes for coverage/variance diagnostics if the underlying tapes support them. Invalidate the lead if reliable one-second outcomes and even generous post-delay ceilings are economically inadequate, or if apparent return exists only before the delayed entry. Report: delay_decay_all_attempts_report, with every opportunity, timing decomposition, cost bridge, all delay cells, uncertainty and concentration diagnostics.

T2. Simple independent burst — primary benchmark

Define external swaps as all observed market swaps other than our own simulated orders, including the teacher’s actual transactions. At a successful whole-transaction post-state, require the last two external swaps to be buys, at least two distinct buyers in the past 250 ms, positive signed executed SOL flow in that interval, and a positive quote-price change over that interval. Use current active supported curves with virtual SOL<=46.171004438 only as a fixed comparability arm to the existing lead. Do not require teacher identity, teacher inventory or later teacher activity. A missing complete recent interval means ineligible with a recorded reason. Use the first observed qualifying opportunity after complete warm-up, with one entry per mint per policy version and a persistent registry. Claim a lifecycle-first crossing only when CREATE-to-present coverage exists.

Entry: decision-ready time+1,000 ms. Primary 0.1 SOL and fixed 15-second hold; 0.5/1 SOL capacity scenarios; exit delivery and costs follow section 5. The exact phase cutoff is teacher-derived, so this is a predeclared candidate, not evidence that the threshold is optimal. A rolling-flow-only variant without launch features is permitted as a separately named policy, not a hidden substitution into the frozen model.

Controls: sample three eligible non-signal mints at the exact decision clock using a deterministic seed and predecision matching strata. Never remove a control because it later signals or falls. Do not reuse the same mint/path as an unpaired independent control; if risk-set controls are reused across signals, retain identities and dependence weights. If three controls are unavailable, keep the opportunity and report control coverage rather than silently excluding its outcome.

Targets: 100 episodes for engineering coverage, then 500 distinct nonoverlapping development episodes across at least seven days to estimate variance/costs. Freeze one version. Confirm on at least 2,000 independent mint opportunities across at least 14 days, increased by the power calculation in section 8. Rejections/failures advance the opportunity denominator. Report: burst_native_fixed15 with net SOL per intended opportunity, filled-trade economics, cash/inventory, matched excess return, daily results, latency/capacity, failure funnel, winner concentration and confidence intervals. Failure to meet causal gates invalidates the run; a confidence interval below the worthwhile effect at a preregistered futility look demotes the policy.

T3. Persistence confirmation — preferred challenger

Start from exactly the T2 seed opportunities. At seed+2 seconds, require at least 50% of the initial positive 250 ms log-price move to remain; positive signed net flow since the seed; at least two new distinct buying wallets; and no buyer contributing more than 60% of buy SOL during confirmation. Freeze these as one proposed rule; wallet breadth is only a heuristic and does not prove independent participants. Evidence already available at confirmation determines the new decision.

Entry is no earlier than seed+3 seconds because the new decision gets its own one-second delay. Use a fixed 30-second full exit with one-second exit delivery; all three sizes and costs as above. Retain every original seed, including confirmation rejects and lost opportunities. Compare its absolute all-seed economics and a feasible portfolio with T2, not merely its conditional winner rate. A paired event comparison is diagnostic because fill times and exposure differ.

Use the same 500-episode development and power-sized fresh confirmation program, with at least 2,000 seed opportunities over 14 days; the actual number of confirmed trades may be much smaller and must drive the precision calculation. Report: persistence_vs_burst with the full confirmation funnel, delayed-entry deterioration, saved/forgone trades, net policy differences, concentration and capacity. Invalidate any advantage that requires using confirmation evidence with the earlier burst entry price. Relative timing robustness is better than a subsecond imitation hypothesis; profit remains unknown.

T4. Sell-shock recovery — a different entry mechanism

After sell-side parity is established, register one long-only recovery family: over a two-second interval, at least two executed sells, negative signed flow, and at least a 5% quote-price drawdown; then a one-second confirmation interval with no new low, positive net flow, and at least two distinct new buyers. Decide only at confirmation end and enter another 1,000 ms later. The mechanism is recovery after observable selling, not prediction of which teacher buys next.

Use an active supported curve, genuine current inventory/liquidity and the section 5 guards. Primary 0.1 SOL, fixed 30-second exit plus one-second delivery, 0.5/1 SOL capacity. Compare same-clock depth/phase/volatility controls and, diagnostically, shocks that do not recover; do not redefine failed confirmations as missing data. Develop on 500 independent shocks, then power-size a separate fresh sample with 2,000-shock/14-day floor. Report: sell_shock_recovery_report with shock/confirmation trajectories, sell coverage, failed reversals, tail losses, all-attempt net economics and control excess. Stop if the effect requires missing sells, non-liquidatable reserves or hindsight low identification.

T5. Slower signals in established PumpSwap pools — conditional venue pivot

If early-curve residual economics fail, test whether later liquidity and slower information improve the cost/latency budget. Admit pools prospectively when already migrated, routeable and continuously observed; never select a historical token because it later migrated or survived. Build exact version-specific pool reserves, fee/retained-fee accounting and signed virtual-quote adjustment before pricing.

A fixed starting arm: at a one-second common-clock tick, positive five-second signed flow, at least five distinct buyers, positive five-second price change, and prospective 1 SOL average curve impact <=0.5%. First qualifying opportunity per mint per UTC day with no overlapping position; cluster all days of a mint together. Entry+1,000 ms, primary 0.1 SOL, full exit+60 seconds then+1,000 ms delivery; 0.5/1 SOL capacity. Do not carry the early-curve phase cutoff into this venue.

Develop on 500 independent pool episodes; confirm with power-sized fresh observations, a 2,000-episode/14-day floor, and disclosure of unique mints/effective sample size. Controls are same-time eligible pools matched on depth/volatility/fees, without matching away flow breadth. Report: venue_feasibility_and_slow_flow with route coverage, opportunity rate, cost floor, signal effect, all-attempt capacity, migration/phase failures and concentration. Demote if deeper liquidity does not yield a better executable cost budget or no incremental net return remains. This ranks below the shared-Pump tests in initial information per engineering time; its parity prospects are a hypothesis, not measured probability.

T6. Exit testing — defer until entry survives

After one entry family has positive delayed fixed-hold evidence, compare its frozen fixed exit with one adaptive alternative: full exit after observed net liquidation return crosses +5% or −5%, otherwise at the original maximum hold. Compute the trigger from sellable value after fees/costs, then apply the same one-second sell delivery and constraints. Do not fill at the threshold price. Keep entry population, size and capital rules fixed. Partial exits should be a later separate hypothesis requiring state mutation and per-leg accounting.

Estimate the paired difference on a separate development block, then freeze and confirm on new data with power based on paired exit differences. Report: frozen_vs_adaptive_exit_report including every identical entry, trigger/fill gap, failure/open-inventory outcomes, paired mean and tail differences, fees and capital duration. No 27-exit winner search on the confirmation cohort. If the entry edge is absent or unresolved, this test is lower priority than abandoning the entry family.

8. Pre-registration, controls, power, and stop rules

Use the right target and sampling unit

The primary target is after-cost, all-attempt net SOL per independently generated eligible opportunity under a fully specified position policy, with unresolved valuation intervals. Also report net return per filled trade and per capital-time, but do not substitute these conditional metrics for the opportunity-level target. A profitable treatment with no control excess may be a broad market exposure; a positive control excess with negative absolute PnL is not a profitable strategy. Require both absolute and incremental evidence.

For the main early-curve study, use one first observed qualifying opportunity per mint per policy version after complete warm-up, with a persistent mint registry and complete hold/delivery windows. Separate an event-response analysis from a feasible portfolio. A concrete initial shadow portfolio is a 10 SOL account, a 1 SOL cost/liquidity reserve, at most three concurrent positions, deterministic earliest-decision priority with mint-ID tie breaking, and no same-mint overlap or borrowing. At each notional, record insufficient-capital and capacity rejections. These are proposed research settings; changing them is a new policy version.

For chronological confirmation, purge or embargo any feature/outcome overlap by at least the maximum lookback plus holding/delivery/retry window. Never split one mint trajectory across development and a purported unseen-mint test. Report separate future-time and never-before-seen-mint generalization where relevant. Resample at the mint/lifecycle level and time-block level; inspect creator/common-funder clustering only as sensitivity, without treating wallet labels as proof of independent participants.

Hash the actual experiment, not a decorative JSON file

Pre-register code and dependency revisions, model/feature schema, decoder/program support, exact input cutoff, available-at semantics, all admission/entry/exit rules, sizes, min-out and impact guards, cost schedule, controls and seed, portfolio constraints, unresolved-value treatment, primary endpoint, alternative arms, power calculation, stopping rule and output schema. Fail if runtime parameters disagree with the contract. A correctness repair that changes decisions or PnL ends that confirmatory version; retain the old run as exploratory.

Log every inspected filter, exit, size, cohort, timing bucket and diagnostic selection in a trial ledger. The 1,701 possible lab cells and 567 latency cells are not independent, but correlation does not erase selection. A fresh capture segment becomes development when it is repeatedly viewed and used to change the rule. Limit confirmation to a few frozen hypotheses and use Holm adjustment or a predeclared dependence-aware max-statistic method. Do not infer a clean holdout from a name or selection_performed=false flag.

Sample targets must follow variance and dependence

The 100/500/2,000 episode milestones are engineering/development/operational floors, not guarantees of statistical significance. For illustration, the two-sided normal approximation n_eff≈(1.96+0.84)^2×sigma_delta^2/delta_min^2 gives about 1,225 independent paired observations for a worthwhile 2% effect with 25% standard deviation, and 4,900 at 50% standard deviation. With a 0.5% effect and 10% standard deviation, it is about 3,136. Multiple testing, serial dependence and heavy tails can require more. Use observed development paired variance and cluster design effects to set the final sample before confirmation.

Measure coverage/variance in development, then choose a fixed sample-and-calendar stopping rule; require both the target count and at least 14 days, with a fixed maximum collection date and an inconclusive result if insufficient. Operations dashboards may monitor health without exposing arm PnL. Any early futility or sequential success rule must have a prespecified error-control method. Do not stop at a profitable peak.

Concrete invalidation and promotion standards

• Invalidate the run if an unavailable input changes an action, a supported-mode reserve/fee mismatch remains unexplained, a failed attempt is lost, or candidate/control coverage differs in an unaccounted outcome-dependent way.

• Demote a family if a planned futility analysis excludes the minimum economically worthwhile effect, plausible predeclared latency/cost stress removes the effect, or delayed-entry economics are negative even under a generous valid ceiling. A non-significant small sample is inconclusive, not proof of zero edge.

• Require the confirmation lower confidence bound to exceed zero for all-attempt net return and matched excess return, with a prespecified minimum effect sufficient to cover amortized operating cost. Show tail losses, worst day, maximum capital occupancy, unresolved inventory, and sensitivity excluding the best mint and best day; do not delete outliers to manufacture a pass.

• If open-position valuation uncertainty can change the conclusion, retain “unresolved.” If results depend on one launch, creator cluster, market day, or optimistic fill assumption, classify them as fragile and require fresh evidence.

• A positive shadow result earns further independent execution validation using observed execution telemetry where available. It does not certify actual order landing or authorize live trading. This audit makes no deployment recommendation.

Statistical rationale: Bailey, Borwein, López de Prado and Zhu, “The Probability of Backtest Overfitting,” reference P6. Specific sample formulas and thresholds here are audit planning calculations, not claims from the supplied trading data.

10. Evidence package and reproducibility

The companion archive includes this report’s structured content and readable text, the original-file hash manifest and code inventory, detailed independent native/scorer/economics workstream reports, preserved probe outputs, and a portable runner. The runner accepts an extracted source root and output directory; it imports supplied modules and executes synthetic assertions without network-facing collectors or trade submissions. It requires the original source bundle, which is not duplicated in the evidence archive.

Synthetic counterexamples prove the stated code behavior for the included version. They do not measure prevalence in production, quantify historical PnL distortion, or establish that every snapshot was deployed. The historical empty-future counterexample is reproduced in the economics probe. The separate size-impact illustration is labeled reference-only because its original exploratory calculation was not packaged as a standalone regression. Re-run the portable suite after extraction and compare with the recorded outputs before relying on a patch.

The audit deliberately leaves the original source unchanged. The fastest useful follow-up is an isolated measurement repair followed by the T1 delayed-edge falsification and the T2/T3 small prospective comparison. Completing the exact teacher classifier can proceed as a benchmark lane; it should not remain the critical path to finding an independently executable strategy.
