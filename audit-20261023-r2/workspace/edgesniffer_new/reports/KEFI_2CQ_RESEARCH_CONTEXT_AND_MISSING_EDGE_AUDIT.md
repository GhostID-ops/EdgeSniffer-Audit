# KEFI_2CQ_RESEARCH_CONTEXT_AND_MISSING_EDGE_AUDIT

Scope: offline research reconstruction, 2026-10-12/13 UTC. No sealed live PRE_H3 actionability outcomes, evaluator output, or PnL was read. No live code, service, boundary, matcher, threshold, runner, or cohort was changed.

## Executive Summary

2CQ established a strong selection-imitation result. The frozen V2 model ranked the teacher-selected mint first at 92.67% on the untouched N150 holdout, with Top-3 100%, MRR 0.948 and mint-balanced concordance 0.9965. This establishes which observed opportunity the teacher chose at the teacher moment. It does not establish autonomous timing, executable latency parity, or profitability.

kEFi established a narrower fact. Historical entries repeatedly occurred in a public PumpSwap pool/flow context, and a frozen 13-feature pre-entry model matched 99 of 150 fresh complete outcome-blind prospective candidates. That establishes a recurring context signature, not an economic edge. The opened H1 result was negative in both fingerprint and matched-control arms and worse in the fingerprint arm. H1 rejects the compound claim recognizable context plus fixed delayed entry plus fixed exit equals tradable edge. It does not prove the context signature false, and it does not recover kEFi sizing, entry order, inventory, re-entry, or exit behavior.

PRE_H3 remains useful as an actionability and data-integrity prerequisite. It cannot resolve the larger missing mechanisms. Its frozen runner has no calendar, maximum-window, or maximum-target terminal condition if the 70% matched-control representation gate never arrives. That is a protocol gap to resolve only outside this live cohort.

## Wallet Ground Truth

### 2CQ

Observed fact: the frozen V2 selection model achieved 92.67% Top-1 and 100% Top-3 on N150. The feature family covers lifecycle, micro-flow, buyer/seller composition, creator participation, recency, and short price movement.

Observed fact: the program separated cross-mint selection from same-mint timing and size/conviction.

Reconstructed estimate: in a non-authoritative N350 sandbox, frozen-model Top-1 had 120 exact evaluable episodes, PF 1.142, total +0.407 SOL, 32.5% wins, median ROI -2.54%, and 9.6% top-winner share of gross profit. This is development context, not validation.

Limitation: the alpha decomposition says actual 2CQ alpha was not reproducible from retained data. Complete realized PnL, holding times, partial exits, re-entry, inventory, and true wallet schedule remain unavailable.

### kEFi

Observed fact: 1,656 BUY entries spanned 150 pools/mints. Repetition is substantial: top pools had 128, 101, 65, 57, and 41 entries. Rows cannot be treated as independent token opportunities.

Observed fact: 1,650 entries had retained 120-second PumpSwap replay. The integrity report has zero duplicate signatures, replay keys, ordering violations, or out-of-window rows. Six entries lacked replay events and remain fail-closed.

Observed fact: the historical sample is a mature PumpSwap regime: median pre-pool quote reserve 81.13 SOL, median fee 120 bps, and median quoted pool change 0.97 SOL in native report units.

Unavailable: the cited corpus does not establish wallet realized PnL, win rate, holding times, partial exits, inventory, re-entry economics, or true exit rules. Captured replay BUY and SELL events are market events, not a wallet-position ledger.

Inference: repeat-pool concentration makes wallet inventory, adding, and re-entry a high-priority plausible mechanism, but not a proven source of profit.

## Implications for PRE_H3 and Later H3

PRE_H3 is useful but only for one component: whether the source/materialization/control pipeline can produce complete delayed trajectories and comparable contemporaneous controls. It cannot validate profitability or recover wallet management. It must continue unchanged.

High confidence:
- 2CQ has validated causal mint-selection evidence.
- Selection, timing, size, and exits are separate questions.
- kEFi has a recurrent public PumpSwap context.
- Current kEFi fingerprint is not an exit or inventory model.

H1 disproved:
- Broad context recognition plus one fixed delayed-entry/fixed-exit convention is sufficient for fresh economics.

Unknown:
- Whether retained evidence can reconstruct kEFi true profitability and exits.
- Which mechanism drives any genuine kEFi edge.
- Whether public evidence arrives early enough for viable execution.

Historical work allowed before a future H3:
- Outcome-blind canonical reconstruction of inventory/re-entry and source-arrival ordering.
- Descriptive clustering of repeat-entry episodes.
- Mechanical fee/slippage and provenance validation.

Work requiring genuinely new prospective evidence:
- Any new entry rule, threshold, control definition, timing rule, exit rule, or economic claim.
- Live-parity execution and dust calibration.

## Conclusion

**CURRENT_DIRECTION_VALID_BUT_INCOMPLETE**

The direction correctly distinguishes public-state recognition from actionability and economics. It is incomplete because H1 shows that broad context matching does not automatically monetize, while lead time, size/impact, wallet state/re-entry, and exits were never represented in the fingerprint. PRE_H3 should proceed unchanged under its frozen contract. A later H3 must preregister one missing mechanism at a time instead of retuning the broad score.
