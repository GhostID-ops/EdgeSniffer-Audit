The grilling section

Questions a technical crypto person will ask.

Use the short answer first, then expand if they want the details.

Is EdgeSniffer live trading right now?

No. The current posture is receive-only and research-only. The infrastructure is being prepared for live parity, but no trading result is being represented as proven live profitability.

So what does the sniffer actually do?

It detects structured on-chain behavior, classifies the real venue and route, reconstructs the relevant market state, and tests whether a decision remains executable after the signal arrives.

Why reverse-engineer a wallet?

A high-activity wallet provides labeled examples of decisions worth studying. We analyze historical windows and live behavior to identify repeatable conditions; the finished sniffer should be independently selective, not permanently dependent on that wallet.

Are the 12 fingerprints 12 profitable strategies?

No. They are named research lanes. Each one isolates a venue or route so results cannot be mixed. A lane becomes a strategy candidate only after exact decoding, causal economics, controls, and prospective validation.

Why does a one-second delay matter?

Because copying a wallet is not instantaneous. The wallet may have received a better price or changed the market before an independent system can act. The test asks what remains after the signal’s real arrival boundary.

How do you prevent look-ahead bias?

Entry state is restricted to information available at the decision boundary. Exit marks occur only after entry. Candidate selection is separated from fresh prospective validation, and unknown venue/state data fails closed.

What makes the old paper PnL not enough?

Paper PnL can be optimistic if it uses a delayed or idealized price. The corrected research explicitly models delay, fees, impact, slippage, failed trades, and missing exit marks. A positive chart is a hypothesis until it survives those constraints.

Why not just use a generic DEX or Jupiter fallback?

Generic routing can hide the actual mechanics and distort the experiment. EdgeSniffer keeps venue-specific decoders and local execution paths. The expected posture is Rust-only execution with Jupiter fallback disabled.

Can it handle 0.5 or 1 SOL trades?

That is exactly why the economics surface runs 0.1, 0.5, and 1.0 SOL separately. A strategy that works only at tiny size is not the same product as one that survives realistic size and liquidity.

What happens when a route cannot be decoded?

It is retained as evidence, classified as unresolved, and excluded from economic conclusions. This is slower than guessing, but it protects the research from false positives.

What is the biggest technical moat?

The combination of low-latency observation, wallet/route lineage, exact venue state, causal timing, and a growing library of audited adapters. Each new validated route makes the system broader without contaminating earlier evidence.

How close are you to live trading?

The research stack is close to the next engineering gates: native timing telemetry, quote/live-parity paper evidence, and dust-size execution calibration. “Close” means the infrastructure is converging; it is not a promise of a launch date or profitability.

What would invalidate the project?

If exact prospective, live-parity samples repeatedly fail to beat controls after realistic costs, the corresponding lane is rejected. The architecture can continue across other populated lanes, but no weak result is hidden by blending it with another venue.

What is the simple one-sentence pitch?

EdgeSniffer is an execution-aware on-chain research engine that learns repeatable wallet behavior, translates it into venue-specific fingerprints, and only promotes signals that survive real timing and economic constraints.
