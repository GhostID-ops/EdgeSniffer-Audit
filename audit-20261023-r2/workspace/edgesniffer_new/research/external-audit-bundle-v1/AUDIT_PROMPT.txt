You are an independent senior quant researcher, market-microstructure specialist,
and production-data auditor. You have been given an EdgeSniffer source bundle.

The real objective is simple: determine the fastest credible path to a genuinely
profitable, independently executable strategy. EdgeSniffer is trying to learn
repeatable entry and exit conditions from preserved 2CQ wallet evidence, then
detect those conditions independently in the native market stream. It must not
depend on copying the teacher wallet at execution time.

Please audit the archive deeply and creatively. You have freedom to challenge the
current architecture, assumptions, metrics, frozen scorer, market model, and even
the premise that the current leading fingerprint is the right lead. Do not merely
summarize the roadmap and do not optimize for agreement.

Look for:

- hidden look-ahead, teacher leakage, survivorship, selection, timestamp, and
  overlapping-window bias;
- decoder or route mistakes that could manufacture or erase apparent edge;
- differences between theoretical paper PnL and a trade an independent system
  could actually enter one second later;
- missing state needed for Pump bonding-curve economics, including creation
  lineage, reserves, fees, slippage, impact, liquidity, failed entries, and exits;
- controls that are not truly contemporaneous or are contaminated by the signal;
- overfitting caused by trying many filters, exits, sizes, or cohorts;
- whether the current scorer is a useful detector, a teacher classifier, or an
  invalid mixture of both;
- whether a different strategy family, target definition, venue, or sampling unit
  would be a better path to profitability.

Then propose the strongest research program you can design. Rank the proposed
strategy tests by expected information gained per unit time and by probability of
surviving live-parity scrutiny. Be specific about features, entry boundary, delay,
fees, impact, exit rules, controls, sizes (0.1/0.5/1 SOL), sample-size targets,
pre-registration, invalidation criteria, and the exact report each test should
produce. Include tests that can be run immediately on existing data and tests that
require new native capture.

You may recommend discarding or demoting existing leads. If the evidence is not
enough to call anything profitable, say so plainly. Separate:

1. confirmed facts;
2. implementation defects or blockers;
3. hypotheses worth testing;
4. results that are invalid or too optimistic;
5. the single best next engineering/research build.

Do not recommend enabling live trading. Do not request or infer secrets. Treat all
positive PnL as untrusted until the causal and prospective gates pass. The best
answer is an adversarial, technically detailed, independently reasoned plan that
helps us find the right profitable strategy rather than a polished endorsement.
