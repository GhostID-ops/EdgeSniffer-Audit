What it does

It copies the best traders —
before the trade is gone.

On Solana, the most profitable wallets trade in the open — anyone can see what they buy. The edge isn't knowing what they bought. It's getting the same entry before the price runs. By the time a human notices, or a slow bot reacts through a public API, the move is already over.

Obsidian Labs is the engine that closes that gap. You pick the wallets worth following. It watches them every moment, catches their trade the instant it touches the chain, mirrors it from your wallet in the same block window — then manages the exit so a winner doesn't fade into a loss.

You pick who to copy

Paste any Solana wallet — or use a vetted preset. The bot follows their every buy and sell, live.

It mirrors in milliseconds

Their trade is detected pre-confirmation and copied from your wallet, sized to your rules — not theirs.

It manages the exit

Stop-loss, trailing steps and take-profit run automatically. No staring at charts at 3am.

Under the hood

How the engine actually works.

The same four stages run on every single trade — start to finish in well under a second.
01

Detect

A colocated ShredStream feed plus gRPC and a Rust overlay catch the tracked wallet's trade before the block is even confirmed.

02

Plan

The engine decodes exactly what they bought, picks the fastest native route, and sizes your order under your safety caps.

03

Execute

Your transaction is built, signed locally, and broadcast through Sender + Jito — landing in the same block window.

04

Manage

Stop-loss, trailing steps, laddered take-profit and mirror-exits run automatically until the position closes.

Why it wins

Faster than every retail bot —
and we can prove it.

Maestro, Trojan, Photon, BonkBot — they're custodial Telegram apps that read the chain through the same slow public APIs and skim 1% of every trade. Here's where this engine is different.
01

Pre-confirmation detection

We read Jito ShredStream — the trade is caught in the block's shreds before it's even confirmed. Retail bots poll an enriched API that trails the chain by hundreds of milliseconds.

02

Native routes, no middleman

Direct pump.fun, PumpSwap & Raydium integration decoded to the account level. We skip the aggregator that adds round-trips and flat-out fails on brand-new launches.

03

Sub-millisecond decode

From signal to broadcast our code runs in ~20 ms — decode itself under 1 ms. Everything after that is Solana's own confirmation, which every bot pays equally.

04

You own it — non-custodial

Self-hosted, keys never leave your machine, 0% platform tax. Retail bots hold your keys and take 1% forever.
